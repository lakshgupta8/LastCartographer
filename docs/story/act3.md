## 2. Corvin's argument (bible 9.2: "persuade Corvin, dialogue built from 5.3, 5.4, 5.6")
Three rounds, one answer right in each. The fight (6.14) happens whichever way it goes; the stance is what he does after.

| Round | Corvin | The answer that moves him | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.3 | "Ilse fades. Do you want your mother to fade?" | "She let me go. She'd let this go." | Ilse heard (`blank.ilse.heard`); dimmed without |
| 5.4 | "The Hollow went under because I held. And you would have me hold nothing?" | "You held alone. That pulled it under." | Nothing but listening: Idrenne's Fire said it ("one town can't do it alone") |
| 5.6 | "Break the frame and it scatters. Nobody holds it whole again." | "It came back to me. Not the Atlas." | The Wind Gate's leap (`windreach.leap.done`); dimmed without |

All three: "You are young, and you might be right. I am old. Show me." (`corvin.stance` = 2). Any miss: "You are young,
and you are wrong, and I love you. Sit." (3). "You couldn't have known" is a trap: he does not say "it wasn't my fault",
and will not let her say it for him.

Corvin's offer follows bible 9.1 exactly: in the Fixed World **nobody flies**, because the Atlas holds the sky for
them. His line is "The sky stays held, safe, for everyone."

## 3. The Rest (bible 9.4)
After the fight, if Wren carries no stones at all, Corvin offers the chair: "Stay. Sit in the chair. Draw with me."
She can leave his stone in his talons and keep the count at zero (`ending.rest_offered`, no `keystone.archivist`).
The Rest also asks for zero anchors; counting them is DES-12's.

## 4. Voss says it once
Voss does not say "Corra" (character-bibles.md §8, and not at the Threshold in Act 2). At the Return, if Wren carries
Corra's small drawing, he says it: "Corra." Once. That is the change, and it is the only time the rule breaks. He goes
in to find her (9.5). If she does not carry it: "We will not. Somebody must hold the line." A statue is NAR-13's.

## 5. Marrow's words
The rule (character-bibles.md §5): echoes of the scene, and one original word per island that nobody in the scene
says. This arc fixes three: **"Wren."** (the Hollow: nobody there has her name; Ilse never had it, and Isolde calls
her "journeyman"), **"Up."** (the capital, after Corvin's "nothing was lost"), **"Look."** (the Return, after the Guildmaster
who never looked). The fourth, the verdict, is NAR-13's. The Unwritten repeats the third, so its last word is "Look."
The Act 3 tests hold Ilse's scene free of "Wren".

## 6. Who says what
- **Isolde**: the pages, plain; in the Hollow, proud ("journeyman"), and does not say "I'm sorry".
- **Ilse**: lets go; sees her first; does not say "stay"; does not have Wren's name.
- **Corra** (heron chick; added to the cast): draws him bigger; "Papa. And Aurelian, when he's in trouble." Does not say
  "gone".
- **Corvin**: "When I—"; grand, weary, self-correcting.
- **Pell**: one item. Surveyor, "The leaves"; Warden, "Her."; Drift, "The sky."
